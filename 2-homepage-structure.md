# 2. Homepage structure

The principle: someone lands here and within seconds understands who Stud Von Axe is, what they sell, and how to ask about it. Horse people scan, they do not read.

Everything connects. The homepage answers what they do, why they do it, who they are, and what is available right now.

## Sections, in order

### 1. Hero
Full width. Their own foal or horse photography, or video if the material allows. Logo, one line that says what they are, and two clear actions: view the foals and embryos, and contact.

Keep the text short. One statement, not a paragraph.

### 2. Who they are
Short. Three or four sentences maximum. Ontario, the base near Lanaken, foals born and raised there and sold from there. A link out for anyone who wants more, but the homepage version stays brief. They explicitly did not want a heavy about page.

### 3. The three business lines
Three entry points, equal weight, clearly separated:
- Foals
- Embryos, with frozen and implanted as separate routes underneath
- Semen sales through Avantea

The semen line is new and has never been on the site. Give it the same visual weight as the others, not a footnote.

### 4. Available now
A selection of current foals and embryos pulled forward onto the homepage. This is what most visitors came for.

Card design matters here. Each card needs: image, genetics line, year of birth, category, and status. Filters live on the overview page, not here, but the cards should already show which categories exist (colt or filly, jumping or dressage, frozen or implanted).

Sold status: design something better than a corner flag. Try a few options across the three directions so the client has a choice. A subtle overlay, a change in card treatment, a small typographic label. Show variety.

### 5. Semen sales
Its own band. Avantea, the stallions available, and a clear route to enquire. New business line, so it needs a proper introduction rather than a list.

### 6. Services
One compact band covering the rest: sourcing horses for clients, mediation, mares available for sport and breeding. Short. This is not a main pipeline, it just needs to exist so people know it is possible.

### 7. News and results
They post results of horses they bred and sold. Strong proof and worth keeping. Two or three recent items, linked out.

### 8. Contact
Simple form, short. Name, email, message. Plus a WhatsApp button, which the client raised themselves as something they liked.

### 9. Footer
Navigation, language switcher, social links, contact details. Space for HorseTelex and Hippomundo brand links.

## Things to get right

**Request buttons everywhere.** On every card and in every band there should be an easy route to ask about a horse. This is a conversion site, not a brochure.

**The gallery.** Where images appear together, they scroll. The client specifically disliked images sitting as separate blocks. This applies mainly to the detail pages but show the intent on the homepage.

**Language switcher.** Visible, not hidden in the footer only. Italian and English at launch, more later.

**Mobile.** Most of their audience is on a phone at a show. Design mobile first for the card sections.
