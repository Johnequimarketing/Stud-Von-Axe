Homepage concepts

Three directions, one hero

All three share the same header and hero, which is signed off. What changes below the fold is the typography, the spacing and the way navy and gold carry the structure. Open each on your phone as well as your laptop, the card sections are designed mobile first.

Direction AClassic

Traditional European stud. Restrained and timeless, generous white space, fine navy rules between sections, gold only on small labels and hover. A numbered directory replaces the usual row of three cards for the business lines.

Open concept A ↗

Direction BModern editorial

Magazine feel. Strong contrast between headline and body, asymmetric layouts, full bleed imagery, navy used as thin frames rather than filled panels so all reading stays on light ground. One foal gets a feature sized card.

Open concept B ↗

Direction CContemporary refined

The most modern of the three. Disciplined grid, small caps labels, navy as full structural bands with white cards on top, gold reserved almost entirely for status and interaction. The horses sit in a horizontal scrolling gallery rather than a static grid.
