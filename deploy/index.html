Homepage concept

Contemporary refined

The most developed of the three directions explored, and the one carried forward. Disciplined grid, small caps labels, navy as full structural bands with white cards on top, gold reserved almost entirely for status and interaction. The horses sit in a horizontal scrolling gallery rather than a static grid, which was a deliberate response to the client disliking images sitting as separate blocks. Open it on your phone as well as your laptop, the card sections are designed mobile first.

Direction CContemporary refined

Two bases, live results styled as a feed, an Instagram section, and HorseTelex plus Hippomundo links on every horse, benchmarked against six other stable sites the agency has built.
