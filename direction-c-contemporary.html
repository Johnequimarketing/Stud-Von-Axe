02 Who we are
VA

Stud Von Axe started with a plain love of horses and grew into years spent studying which bloodlines actually hold up in sport. We chose every broodmare in our herd after watching her pedigree and her offspring for seasons, not weeks.

We collect and freeze embryos out of Ontario, then bring them home to our base near Lanaken in Belgium, where our vet team manages the implants, carries the pregnancies and raises the foals until they are ready to sell.

Clients who work with us get a straight answer, before the sale and after it. That is the whole basis of the relationship.
