# 1. Client briefing and brand guidelines

## Who the client is

Stud Von Axe is an Italian sport horse breeder based in Desenzano del Garda. They produce frozen embryos in Ontario, and run a second base in Belgium close to Lanaken where their vet team implants embryos, manages the pregnancies and where the foals are born and raised before being sold.

Three business lines:
- Foals and embryos, split into frozen embryos and implanted embryos. This is the core.
- Semen sales through Avantea. New, and not on the current website at all.
- Some sport horse activity and sourcing horses for clients. Secondary, mentioned but not a main pipeline.

They sell mostly direct, not through auctions. The website brings in a large share of their clients. In their own words it is the postcard of the business.

Current site: studvonaxe.it, built in 2019. Structure today is Home, About Us, Sport Horses, Foals, Broodmares, Embryos, Contacts.

## What they said in the meeting

**On the look.** The current site is not elegant enough and not professional enough for where the business sits now. They want it modern, elegant, following current standards. Their name is growing as a breeder and the site has to grow with it.

**On the brand.** They keep the logo, the dark blue and the gold. The gold changes: they are sending a new Pantone, the one they use on Instagram stories. Not the current orange tone.

**On readability.** This is the specific complaint. Text on dark blue backgrounds is hard to read and feels noisy. Their own suggestion: white backgrounds, blue as a border or frame, gold as the accent.

**On typography.** The current font is too strong and not elegant. They want something more refined. They explicitly asked to see options ranging from classic to modern, and said they are open to something more extravagant than they would pick themselves.

**On the homepage.** They want a short introduction to who they are on the homepage. Not a heavy separate About page. A brief explanation is enough.

**On images and video.** Images should scroll in a gallery, not sit as separate blocks. Video needs to be presented properly inside the page.

**On horse and embryo pages.** The current detail page feels chaotic. Genetics and information need a calmer, organised layout that holds attention. This is not part of the homepage task, but the card design on the homepage should already point at where the detail page is going.

**On sold status.** They want to keep showing what is sold. They do not like the current flags. Show them something different.

**On pedigree links.** Both HorseTelex and Hippomundo links on every horse. Some clients prefer one, some the other. They see it as extra service. The Hippomundo blacktype presentation also needs updating.

**On managing it themselves.** They update the site constantly, adding horses and photos. Everything has to be quick to edit. Not part of this task, but keep it in mind: no design that only works with a perfect photo.

**On languages.** Italian and English at launch, French and German possible later. Design has to survive longer German and French words in navigation and buttons.

## Brand rules

Keep: the logo, the dark blue, gold as accent.

Change: the gold tone (new Pantone coming), the typography, the balance between dark and light.

Their instruction on colour use: lighter backgrounds as the base, blue for structure and framing, gold as accent only. Do not build large blocks of text on dark blue.

## What is still missing

Logo files in vector, and the new gold Pantone. Both are coming from the client. Until they arrive, pull the blue and gold from the current site as placeholders and put every colour in a CSS variable so the swap takes one minute.
