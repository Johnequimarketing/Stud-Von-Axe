04 · Who we are

Born from a passion for horses in Desenzano del Garda, on Italy's Lake Garda.

Years spent studying which bloodlines actually hold up in sport led us to choose every broodmare in our herd on pedigree and produce, not on a season's form. We collect and freeze embryos out of Ontario, then bring them to our base near Lanaken in Belgium, where our vet team manages the implants, carries the pregnancies and raises the foals until they are ready to sell.
