## Photo findings from the build (13 August)

Card images were pulled from the client's own listing pages. Two are worth raising with Mark:

- **Hayley VD Berghoeve Z** — her listing thumbnail on studvonaxe.it is a competition photo whose filename is `8578-H5-Ganesh-Hero-Z-Carlos-Hank-Guerreiro-...`, i.e. a photo of a different horse (Ganesh Hero Z). Hayley is a 2024 broodmare and would not be competing. This is the client's own published pairing, not our error, but in a polished concept it reads as a mistake.
- **Mosito x Carma VD Berghoeve Z** (frozen embryo) — listing uses `Elvis-Ter-Putte-5.jpg`, again a different named horse.

**Decision, 13 August:** the concepts show only the four horses whose photographs actually match the named horse (Arkhana, Cortina de Jolie, Charina, Unguessable). Hayley and the Mosito embryo are held back until the client supplies correct images. Ask Mark to request those two photos.

Both point at a real design question for the new site: **what does an embryo card show?** There is no photo of an embryo, so the card needs a deliberate answer (sire photo clearly labelled as the sire, damline photo, or a typographic card with no image at all). Worth putting to the client as a design decision rather than letting them keep filling the slot with unrelated competition shots.
