Concept 1, Asymmetric triptych. Never three equal boxes. Foals gets the tall photograph because it is the core business and the one with the strongest real image. Embryos gets a smaller photograph of the facility. Semen gets no photograph at all, a quiet oversized numeral sits behind the text instead, since there is no real image for it and a stock photo of a lab or a syringe would be the fastest way back to looking generic.
