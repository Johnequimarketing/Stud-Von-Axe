# 7. Logo selection

Client sent four renders of one mark. It is a horse skull silhouette facing forward, split by a vertical line with three dots, a crown on top, wordmark VON | AXE either side, with a rule line and "BREEDING AND / HORSE TRADING" tagline underneath.

The four files are one mark in a 2x2 matrix: light background or dark background, gold accent or monochrome.

| File | Background | Crown / accent | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Image 1 | White / light | Gold crown, gold rules, navy mark | **Primary — light backgrounds** |
| Image 2 | Navy / dark | Gold crown, gold rules, white mark | **Primary — dark bands** |
| Image 3 | Navy / dark | White crown, white rules, white mark | Monochrome reserve |
| Image 4 | White / light | Grey crown, navy mark | Monochrome reserve |

## Decision

- **Image 1** is the main logo for anywhere on a light background — which is most of the site per the client's own brand rule (light backgrounds carry content, gold as accent only). Gold only touches the crown and the two rule lines, nothing more, exactly matching their instruction.
- **Image 2** is the reversed version for navy bands (footer, dark hero overlays, any full navy section in Direction B or C). Keeps the same gold accent moment so the crown stays the one consistent signature whether the section is light or dark.
- **Images 3 and 4** (monochrome) are reserve assets, not for the homepage directly. Useful for: favicon, embroidery or single colour print, a subtle watermark over a photo, social avatar where colour can't be trusted to render consistently.

## Flag for Mark

All four files are JPEGs with a solid background baked in, not the vector files the brief calls for. Fine for deciding which version wins, not fine for production — the logo needs to sit on top of photos (hero image, card overlays) without a visible box around it. Ask Mark to get PNG (transparent) or SVG/AI versions of Image 1 and Image 2 specifically from the client. Vector also matters for the favicon and any large format use (print, signage) down the line.
