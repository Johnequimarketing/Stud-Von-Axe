02 Welcome to Stud Von Axe

Born from a passion for horses.

Years spent studying which bloodlines actually hold up in sport led us to choose every broodmare in our herd on pedigree and produce, not on a season's form. We breed and sell foals and embryos from our base in Italy, with embryos produced in Ontario and implanted at our second base near Lanaken in Belgium.
