Feedback and audit log

Every correction, and the check that catches it next time.

Mark's feedback across the build, in his own words, turned into something checkable, and what he keeps choosing, so the next design starts closer to right. A rule that only lives in a conversation gets broken again; a rule with a check behind it does not. This page is read before starting and run through before handing anything back.

How to use it. Run node scripts/audit-homepage.mjs <file> first, which runs twenty one checks on a public page. Then walk the items tagged by eye, because a script cannot tell whether a sentence is true. Nothing goes to Mark with a red line in the audit.

What he keeps choosing

Not corrections. This is taste, read off six days of decisions, so a first draft starts closer to what he would have picked anyway. Where he has chosen the same thing twice it is written as a default; where he chose once it is written as a lean. Read this before designing, not after.

Contained and rounded, never edge to edge

Every single time. wilde de ronde border radius houden contained in het scherm, and the header became a plate on the page gutter rather than a band across the screen. A full bleed band was tried twice and rejected twice.

Default: hold to --wrap and the gutter, round with --plate-radius, and let the plate hang or cut rather than span.

Taste

A photograph under a plate, not a photograph beside a paragraph

He picked the backdrop variant, the plate with a photograph in it, and the invitation with a picture behind the veil, three times out of three. bouw deze in met achtergrond foto erin.

Default: navy plate, photograph behind it, one-colour veil, type on top. The veil is for a plate; it is never laid over the hero to make the header readable.

Taste

Movement, but quiet

He asked for the hero to move on its own, and on the about page: ik wil de foto fade naar andere foto's hebben en wat dynamisch, vind het beetje saai zo. He has never asked for a carousel, a bounce or a parallax.

Default: a crossfade on a five to six second beat, pausing on hover and focus, off entirely under prefers-reduced-motion, with a way to take control by hand.

Taste

A section carries one idea, and something breaks the run

The invitation was moved between the foals and the embryos on his instruction: zet de call to action tussen de foals en embryo's in, maak het mooier, en breekt het een beetje.

Default: two long runs of cards get a plate between them. A section that is trying to do two things is two sections.

Taste

Serif heading, one italic accent word, and the accent is gold

Chosen in the first direction and never questioned since. The menu joined it on 29 Aug: de menu items mogen ook in de fonts van de website.

Default: Fraunces for headings and the menu, Manrope for labels and body, exactly one <em> per heading, gold. Never a third face.

Taste

Gold is an accent, and it carries navy ink

He asked for the contact button in the accent colour, and for the palette to be checked against the logo files rather than judged by eye.

Default: gold for the pill, the accent word and the eyebrow. Text on gold is navy. Gold on ivory only at heading size, where 3:1 is the bar.

Taste

Plain words, and no sentence that has to be defended

gewoon simpele taal houden, geen onzin, and about a headline he had not asked for: what the horses have done, weet je niet, misschien is het wel heel ander nieuws?

Default: say the thing. If a sentence would need a source and has none, it does not go on the page, however well it reads.

Taste

Six variations, genuinely different, in their own file

This is how he wants options: kun je hiervoor 6 variaties maken en mij presenteren?, allemaal verschillend van elkaar. He then names one, and only that one gets built.

Default: a separate HTML with six, each a different idea rather than six spacings of the same idea, in the page's own tokens so the choice is honest. Then build the one he names, and nothing beside it.

Taste

Show the proof, not the claim

He asks for it back: renders, measurements, the audit line. A statement that something works has never once satisfied him, and twice it was wrong.

Default: measure in the browser, render the state, run the audit, and put the number in the answer. Dutch in conversation, English on the page.

HardAutomated

Type on a photograph is measured behind the type, not near it

The four cards on the about page looked fine. Sampled behind the gold label itself, on the rendered page with the text hidden, the brightest card came in at 1.53:1 where the house threshold for gold on a dark ground is 3:1. Sampling a rectangle roughly where the text sits gave 2.8:1 and would have passed it.

Checked: render the state, hide the text, read the label's own bounding box out of the browser and sample that, at every width the layout changes at. Never lighten the gold to pass.

And the fix that holds: hang the dark foot on the TEXT, not on the card. A gradient measured in percentages of the card looks right on a wide screen and fails on a narrow one, because the copy wraps onto more lines while the card gets shorter, so the label rides up into the bright half. Measured at seven widths, the same card ran from 53% to 28%. A gradient on the text block, with the whole fade inside its top padding, is as tall as the words are at every width and survives the copy being rewritten. Cards now measure 3.77:1 at worst, gold, across 390 to 1440.

The mistakes, and what now catches them

Every one of these cost time in this build. They are listed so the check is obvious rather than the lesson being memorable.

What went wrongWhyThe check now
540 lines of CSS deleted in one edit A cut between two markers assumed they were adjacent. The first .hz__ph was in the horses block, the last was an override at the end of the sheet, and everything between them went with it. A region cut must prove what it does not contain, plus a maximum length. Brace balance after every CSS edit.
The about photo vanished, twice over replace(x, y, 1) hit the first occurrence in the file, which was in the about navigator rather than in the embryo data it was meant for. Assert count(x) == 1 before replacing, or match on a longer, unique string.
Background photographs dropping into the layout, three times .x > * carries the same specificity as .x__bg and comes later, so it overrode position:absolute and the image became a flow item. Any > * rule that sets position or z-index must exclude the absolutely placed children with :not().
A heading almost invisible on navy The reset colours h1 to h4 with the ink colour, which beats a colour the parent merely passes down. Every heading on a dark panel states its own colour.
An invisible element still taking space, three times opacity:0 hides; it does not collapse. Nor does a margin on a zero height box, and an inline element still generates a line box. Collapsed means max-height:0 and margin:0 together, and measure the rendered height rather than trusting the source.
A shadow cut off in a straight line overflow-x:auto makes the block axis a scrollport too, so it clips vertically. A card run gives its shadow room in padding and hands the surplus back with a negative margin.
The page scrolling sideways by 1360px A grid item defaults to min-width:auto, so a scroll track stretched its column instead of scrolling inside it. min-width:0 on grid and flex children that hold a track. Check scrollWidth > innerWidth in the browser after every layout change.
Card text running together on one line A span inside a card is inline. The whole card is one link, which cannot contain block elements, so the block behaviour has to be set back deliberately. Every styled span in a card gets display:block.
The contrast check never ran on this page The palette parser expected an unindented :root block and different token names, so it failed silently on every run for weeks. The parser is indentation tolerant and normalises names; skipped pairs are now reported.
Four news pages shipped with links that 404 The cards used relative hrefs. A browser resolves those against the DIRECTORY of the current URL, and with trailingSlash:false the archive is served at /news, whose directory is /. So href="story" resolved to /story. Every internal link on a subpage is root absolute, and the audit fails on any relative one. Verified by fetching each page and every link on it, not by reading the markup.
A screenshot showing a stale page The browser pane returned occluded or cached frames. Render with chrome-headless-shell, and pin viewport-height sections in a temporary copy so svh does not stretch in a tall window.

What the audit covers now

Twenty three checks, run with node scripts/audit-homepage.mjs index.html. Green is the condition for handing anything back, not a nice extra.

CheckCatches
Raw hex outside :rootA colour that should have been a token. Alpha values and mask stops are exempt: those are treatments.
Opaque rgb() outside :rootThe same, for the other notation.
Font familiesA typeface named directly instead of through a token.
Contrast, ten pairsText that fails WCAG AA on its own ground.
Long dashes, spaced hyphens, dash rangesThe punctuation the brief bans.
Filler wordsMarketing language that says nothing.
EyebrowsA number or a dash in front of one.
Logo groundsA reversed logo on a light ground, or a missing data-ground.
Drawn marksA hand-made crown or wordmark standing in for the brand.
In-page anchorsA link to a section that no longer exists.
Duplicate idsTwo elements sharing an id, which breaks label pairing silently.
Labels and fieldsA label pointing at nothing, or a field with no label.
Placeholder photographsA stand-in frame with no Photo pending label.
House wordsWords that are ours rather than this market's, with the better word named. pairing becomes cross, mediation becomes brokering.
Counts in copyLists every number stated, for a human to check against the data. A number that can change should be counted at render time instead.
Heading structureMore than one h1, or a level skipped.
Search headA missing title, description, canonical, social card or structured data. Warns while noindex is on.

Before handing anything back

A new check is proven against the bug it was written for, by reintroducing that bug and watching the check fail, before it is trusted.

The audit runs green on the file that changed, and on every page that inherits from it. A change to index.html is a change to six pages: rebuild them (node scripts/build-news.mjs and node scripts/build-about.mjs) and audit them all, or the site is consistent only in the one file that was looked at.

The browser reports no console errors and no sideways scroll.

The change is measured in the browser, not judged from the source.

Every new claim in the copy has a named source.

Rendered at 1440 and at 390, and both were actually looked at.

Open questions for the client are listed, not silently decided.

Publishing: since 28 Aug he has asked for the work to go live, so the reply states what was pushed, what is live and on which URL, with the status codes checked. Before that date the rule was the opposite, and outside what he has asked for it still is: nothing is committed, pushed or published on our own judgement.

Kept alongside the design system and the build checklist. When new feedback arrives it is added here first, and only then acted on.
