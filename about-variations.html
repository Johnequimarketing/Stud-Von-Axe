V1 · Tabbed narrative
02 Who we are

V1 Tabbed. Three short titles instead of one paragraph after another. Clicking a title crossfades in the matching photograph and swaps the text beside it. The dot marker on the active tab is the only ornament, everything else is restraint. Closest in spirit to how the page already reads, just given a reason to click.

V2 · Accordion
02 Who we are

V2 Accordion. All three headings sit visible at once, so a visitor sees the shape of the whole story before choosing where to look closer. Opening one pushes a small photograph in beside its paragraph. The most familiar interaction of the five, which is a point in its favour, nobody has to learn how it works.

V3 · Big image switcher, same language as the hero
02 Who we are

V3 Big image switcher. The same click to crossfade mechanic as the hero's horse selector, applied here instead of introduced fresh. A visitor who has already used the hero understands this instantly. My pick of the five, for that reason specifically, a page reads as considered when its interactions repeat rather than multiply.

V4 · Flip cards
02 Who we are

V4 Flip cards. Photograph on the front, the paragraph on the back, click to turn it over. The most playful of the five and the one I would push back on hardest for this brand: a physical flip gesture reads closer to a product feature tile than a stud farm's own story, and it risks feeling like a trick the second time a visitor sees it.

V5 · Journey line
02 Who we are

V5 Journey line. Frames the three facts explicitly as a sequence, started, breeds, promises, with a marker line above the image. Earns its place only because the middle fact genuinely is a process with a direction, Ontario to Lanaken. Weakest fit of the five for the first and third facts, which are not really steps in a line, they are two separate ideas being forced into one shape.
