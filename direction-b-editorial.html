Who we are

Stud Von Axe started with a straightforward love of horses, not a business plan. That love turned into years of studying the international bloodlines that actually produce sport horses, not just nice looking ones, and it shaped how we chose every broodmare we own.

We run our embryo work out of Ontario, and our second base sits near Lanaken in Belgium, where embryos are implanted, pregnancies are managed, and foals are born, raised and prepared for sale under our own eyes.

We tell buyers what we actually see in a horse, strengths and limits both, because that is the only way people keep coming back to us after the sale.
