Who we are

A stud built on bloodlines, not on shortcuts.

Stud Von Axe began with a simple love of horses, and grew into years spent studying the bloodlines that produce them. We chose our broodmares with real discipline, looking for mares that pass on both movement and mind, not just a name on a page. Our embryos are produced in Ontario and carried to term at our second base near Lanaken in Belgium, where every foal is born, raised and prepared before it is offered for sale. We tell clients what we know and what we do not, because a horse bought on trust is the only kind worth buying.

Embryos
