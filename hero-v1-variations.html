Part two

How the cards look

Five treatments on the same dark ground the cards actually sit on, so you are judging them in context. Status is text in the metadata in every one, never on the photograph. Pick a text position from part one and a card style here, for example T3 plus S4.

S1 · BareNo container at all. Photo, name, metadata. The quietest option and the closest to how a gallery labels work.
S2 · GlassFrosted panel with a fine light border. Lifts the card off a busy photo without adding weight, and it is the most modern of the five.
S3 · HairlineOne pixel frame, no fill, with a rule separating photo from text. Restrained and slightly formal, closest to Direction A.
S4 · LotAuction house treatment. A lot number and a thin rule above each photo. Adds a sense of a curated, numbered list rather than a product grid.
S5 · RowHorizontal, square thumbnail beside the text. Fits far more horses in the same space and is the strongest of the five on a phone.

On the two horses missing. Only four horses appear in these marquees. Hayley and the Mosito embryo are left out on purpose, because on the client's own site their listing photos show different named horses, Ganesh Hero Z and Elvis Ter Putte. A hero marquee is the most visible place on the site to put that mistake.
