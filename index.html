About Stud Von Axe

From the cross
to the day it leaves

We do not buy a pedigree and hope. Every mare here was chosen after years on the best German farms and long research into the families that actually produce sport horses, not the ones that only read well on paper.

Every cross begins in Desenzano del Garda. Our own team in Lanaken implants the embryo, carries the pregnancy and raises the foal until the day it leaves. Nothing is handed to a third party halfway.

We tell a buyer what we see in a horse, the limits as well as the strengths, and we sell direct. Never through auction.
