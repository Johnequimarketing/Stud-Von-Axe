V4 · Cinematic step carousel
03 · The breeding programme

One programme, three steps

Why this one: the most cinematic of the four, and it reframes the three programmes as steps in one process rather than parallel options, which fits the real relationship between them (foals, frozen embryos and implanted embryos are all outputs of the same two bases, not competing choices). Click a dimmed side card or a dot to advance. Real tradeoff: this needs real photography for every step to look this good, and Implanted embryos still has none, so its slide keeps the same numeral placeholder used everywhere else, which will look noticeably plainer next to the other two until the client supplies one.
